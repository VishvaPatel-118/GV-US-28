28 Months of Us

Happy 28 Months, My Bittuu!

My Bittuu, ❤️

Happy 28 Months to us. ❤️

3 May 2023... ek simple Snap request thi je vaat start thai hati, e kyare mari life ni sauthi beautiful story bani gai, eni mane khabar j na rahi. Pehlo message taro, pehli vaat tari... ane aaje pachhal fari ne jovu chhu tyare realize thay chhe ke mara life ma tari entry ek simple coincidence nathi hati. Tu dhime dhime mari life no evo part bani gayo ke aaje tara vagar mari everyday life imagine karvi pan difficult lage chhe. ❤️

Starting ma hu literally ek feeling-less girl hati. Mane koi ni aadat padti j n hati, koi mate evu kai feel thatu j n htu... pan tari aadat padi gai. Actually, aadat karta pan vadhu. Tu mari routine no part nathi, tu mari life no part bani gayo chhe. ❤️

13 months sudhi tu mane choose karto rahyo. Tane chance hato ke tu mane muki ne koi better person choose kari shake, pan tu kyare walk away n thayo. Tu mane samjhto rahyo, mane sudharto rahyo, mane sachvto rahyo ane sauthi important, mane kyare chhodi nathi. ❤️

2 June 2024 na divse apnu relation officially start thayu, pan honestly, apni story ena karta ghani pela thi lakhai rahi hati. E 13 months ma je connection build thayu, je understanding bani, je attachment thai... e badhu apna "us" nu foundation hatu. ❤️

Aa 28 months ni journey perfect nathi rahi. Ghani vaar apde gussa thaya, misunderstandings thai, ek-bijane hurt pan karyo. Ghani vaar gussa ma me kahi didhu ke "have nathi vaat karvi", relation puru karvani vaat pan kari... pan dil thi hu kyare pan tane chhodva mangti n hati. ❤️

Sometimes hu mara emotions ma evu badhu boli jau chhu je hu actually feel pan n karti hou. Ane mane lage chhe ke tu aa vaat mara karta pan vadhare samje chhe. ❤️

Haju pan tari amuk vastu mane hurt kare chhe, ane kadach mari amuk vastu tane pan hurt karti hase. Pan aa badha vachche ek vaat mane hamesha important lagi chhe — apde darek vakhat ek-bijane samjhvano try karyo chhe. Badhu perfect nathi thayu, pan apde ek-bijathi door javana badle fari ek-bija pase aavta rahya chhiye. ❤️

Maybe aa j apna relation ni sauthi beautiful vaat chhe — apde perfect nathi, pan apde ek-bijane samjhi ne, sambhali ne ane sathe rahi ne better banavta jaiye chhiye. ❤️

Tu jyare mane "bchha" ke "betu" kahi ne bolave chhe ne, tyare aaje pan mara face par automatically smile avi jay chhe. Aa words bija koi mate simple hoy shake, pan mara mate ema tari care, affection ane apnapan badhu avi jay chhe. ❤️

Tari ek vaat mane hamesha yaad rehse — apda banne na past ni badhi khabar hova chhata, te mane kyare tari old stories sathe compare nathi kari. Kadach tane aa vaat ketli moti chhe e realize pan nahi hoy, pan mara mate aa trust ane security ni bahu moti feeling chhe. ❤️

Mara looks badlay, maro face change thayo, maro weight gain thayo, maro mood badlay, maro gusso vadhyo, mari overthinking vadhare thai... pan tari feelings kyare badlai nahi. Mari childishness hoy, maro gusso hoy, maro overthinking hoy ke mara unnecessary emotions — tu badhu sahan kari le chhe. ❤️

Ane sauthi beautiful part e chhe ke ghani vaar hu kai bolu ena pela j tu mari aankho joi ne samji jay chhe ke mane shu thayu chhe. Ek nano eye contact ane tu maro mood samji jay... aa connection hu words ma properly explain kari j nathi sakti. ❤️

Tara efforts ni vaat karu to "thank you" pan khub nano word lage chhe. Private place hoy ke public place, tu mane badha same equal respect ane equal love aape chhe. Public ma tari ek simple nazar ma je care hoy chhe ne, e mane secretly etli khushi aape chhe ke kadach hu tyare tane kahi pan n saku. ❤️

Apde loko vachche betha hoy tyare pan apni ek alag j duniya hoy chhe. ❤️ Koi special occasion ni jarur nathi padti. Sometimes bas vaat karvi, sometimes kai khas na bolvu, sometimes ek-bijane jovu, sometimes gussa karvo ane pachi fari normal thai javu... aa badha simple moments j mara favourite memories chhe. ❤️

Mane periods ma chocolate mokalvi, mari dates yaad rakhvi, mari nani-nani vaat notice karvi, hu shu pasand karu chhu e yaad rakhvu... bija loko mate aa huge gestures na hoy, pan mara mate aa j love chhe. ❤️

Because love mara mate mota words karta vadhare nani nani things ma dekhay chhe.

Tu mane notice kare chhe. Tu mane remember kare chhe. Tu mane sambhale chhe. Tu mane samje chhe. Tu mane sachve chhe. ❤️

Tu ek vaar kidhu hatu ke apna relation pela tu tuti ne bhukko thai gayo hato. E sambhadi ne pan mane hamesha ek vaat feel thay chhe ke life ma ghanu badhu joya pachi pan tu mane etli care thi sambhale chhe, etla efforts kare chhe ane mane etlu love aape chhe. Tari aa side mate hu hamesha grateful rahish. ❤️

Aa 28 months ma tu mane love no ek navo meaning samjavyo chhe. Love only good days nathi. Love etle ek-bijana badha versions ne accept karva. Love etle gussa pachi pan samjhvani koshish karvi. Love etle hurt thay pachi pan relationship ne easily give up na karvu. Love etle ek-bijane change karva karta, ek-bijane better banavta javvu. ❤️

Ane honestly, tari sathe hu aaje je person chhu, ema tari ghani badhi jagya chhe. ❤️

Aaje divse ne divse hu tari vadhu close thati jau chhu. Tari aadat padti jay chhe... actually, aadat karta pan vadhu. Tu mari routine no part nathi, tu mari life no part bani gayo chhe. ❤️

Ane ha... loko apni jodi ne dream relation kahe ke na kahe, mara mate apni jodi perfect etle chhe karan ke apde banne 100% perfect nathi. Apne ek-bijane samji ne perfect banavta jaiye chhiye. ❤️

Mane nathi khabar future ma apni story exactly kya sudhi jashe. Life ma aagal ghana badha chapters aavshe. Ghani khushi aavshe, ghana challenges pan aavshe. Pan Bhagwan pase mari ek simple wish chhe — je journey 3 May 2023 thi start thai hati, ena aagal na darek chapter ma pan apde ek-bijane samjhta, sambhalta ane prem karta rahie. ❤️

28 months complete thai gaya. ❤️

Ane hu bas 28 months celebrate nathi karti. Hu e badhi memories celebrate karu chhu je apde aa time ma sathe create kari. E badhi fights, e badha laughs, e badha calls, e badhi random conversations, e badha silent moments, e badha hugs, e badhi misunderstandings ane pachi ek-bijane fari choose karvani moments. ❤️

Aa badhu mali ne apnu "us" bane chhe. ❤️

Bas ek j wish chhe — aa 28 months ek divas 30 months bane, pachi 40, 50... ane aagal ghana badha years. ♾️❤️

Numbers karta pan important e chhe ke darek number ni pachhal apdi same love, same understanding ane ek-bijane choose karvani same feeling hoy. ❤️

Life ma game teva ups and downs aave, pan bas apde ek-bijano haath kyare chhodta nahi. Ek-bijane samjhta rahie, sambhalta rahie ane prem karta rahie. ❤️

Thank you for choosing me when you had every reason to walk away. ❤️

Thank you for staying when I was difficult to understand. ❤️

Thank you for understanding the things I don't always know how to say. ❤️

Thank you for noticing the little things about me. ❤️

Thank you for making me feel loved in ways that sometimes words cannot explain. ❤️

And most importantly...

Thank you for being my Bittuu,
my bchha,

my person. ❤️

Hu badhu hamesha bolti nathi. Ghani vaar mari feelings properly express pan nathi kari shakti. Pan ek vaat hu pure heart thi kahi shaku chhu...

I love you. ❤️

More than I know how to explain.

Ane maybe hu badhu bolti nathi... pan mari aankho tane badhu kahi de chhe. ❤️

3 May 2023 — The beginning. ❤️
