Happy 28 Months, My Bittuu! ❤️

28 months… ane haju pan believe nathi thatu ke ek simple vaat kyare mari life no sauthi beautiful part bani gai. 🥹

Aa 28 months ni journey ma ghana ups and downs aavya, ghani vaar gussa thayo, misunderstandings thai, ek-bijane hurt pan karyo… pan darek vakhat apde ek-bijane samjhi ne, badhu side ma muki ne fari ek-bija pase aavya. 🫶🏻❤️

Ane maybe aa j apna relation ni sauthi beautiful vaat chhe — apde perfect nathi, pan apde ek-bijane samjhi ne, sambhali ne ane sathe rahi ne better banavta jaiye chhiye. ❤️

Tu mane samje chhe, sambhale chhe, sachve chhe ane sauthi important… mane kyare chhodi nathi. 🥹 Tari sathe darek divas mane love no ek navo meaning samjhave chhe.

Tari sathe spend karelo darek nano moment pan mara mate special chhe. Sometimes vaat karvi, sometimes bas ek-bijane jovu, sometimes gussa karvo ane pachi fari normal thai javu… aa badhu j mari favourite part of “us” chhe. 🫠❤️

Bas ek j wish chhe — aa 28 months aagal pan aavi j rite 30, 40, 50 ane ghana badha years ma convert thata rahe. ♾️❤️

Life ma game teva ups and downs aave, pan bas apde ek-bijano haath kyare chhodta nahi. Ek-bijane samjhta rahie, sambhalta rahie ane prem karta rahie. 🫶🏻
